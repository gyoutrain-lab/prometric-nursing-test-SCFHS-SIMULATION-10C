Maternal & Neonatal – Second Test

1. Mastitis caused by E.colia Streptococcus, & V aureus

Correct Answer: A
SCFHS: Mastitis is commonly caused by Staphylococcus aureus; E. coli and Streptococcus may also be involved.

2. A nurse is providing instructions to a mother who has been diagnosed with mastitis. Which statement indicates need for further teaching?

Correct Answer: D
SCFHS: Breastfeeding should be continued to prevent milk stasis.

3. Proper nursing response about organism of mastitis?

Correct Answer: A
SCFHS: Mastitis organisms generally do not harm the healthy newborn.

4. Which statement indicates need for additional education in mastitis?

Correct Answer: D
Stopping breastfeeding worsens mastitis.

5. Which will relieve discomfort due to breast engorgement?

Correct Answer: C
Frequent breastfeeding relieves engorgement.
