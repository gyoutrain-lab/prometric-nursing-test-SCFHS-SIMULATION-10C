SCFHS Prometric Mock Exam

PART 1 – Questions 1 to 30

1. Mastitis caused by E.colia Streptococcus, & V aureus

Correct Answer: B
WHY (SCFHS): Mastitis is most commonly caused by Staphylococcus aureus, not E. coli.
HOW: Bacteria enter through cracked nipples during breastfeeding.
Distractors:
  • A – STR: mixes general infection organisms with breast-specific infection.

2. A nurse is providing instructions to a mother who has been diagnosed with mastitis. Which statement indicates a need for further teaching?

Correct Answer: D
WHY: Breastfeeding should be continued during mastitis.
HOW: Emptying the breast reduces milk stasis and bacterial growth.
Distractors:
  • A – Correct management.
  • B – Symptom relief.
  • C – Supportive measure.

3. A primiparous mother with mastitis asks if the organism will affect the newborn. Best response?

Correct Answer: A
WHY: Mastitis organisms do not harm healthy term infants.
HOW: Breast milk contains protective antibodies.
Distractors:
  • B – NCLEX trap (non-clinical reasoning).
  • C – Incorrect physiology.
  • D – Overgeneralized immunology (STR).

4. Health education for a mother with mastitis: which statement needs further teaching?

Correct Answer: D
WHY: Stopping breastfeeding worsens mastitis.
HOW: Milk stasis increases inflammation and infection risk.
Distractors:
  • A–C – Appropriate care measures.

5. Postpartum breast engorgement causing pain and poor milk flow. Best relief?

Correct Answer: C
WHY: Engorgement is relieved by emptying the breast.
HOW: Frequent feeding improves milk flow and reduces pressure.
Distractors:
  • A – DELAY: increases discomfort.
  • B – Support only.
  • D – UNSAFE if breastfeeding intended.
